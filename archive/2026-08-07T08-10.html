DISCOUNT, WHICH ORDER — DON'T ASK ME AGAIN

Trade discount comes off the list price first. Settlement discount comes off the trade figure, never off list. A supplier who quotes "33⅓ and 5" means: knock a third off list, then five per cent off what's left. Not thirty-eight and a third off. It is not the same number and it never has been.

$60.00 list, 33⅓ and 5 → $40.00 → $38.00. Somebody doing it the other way gets $37.00 and then wonders why the statement won't come right at the end of the month.
