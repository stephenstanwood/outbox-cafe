Things Col told me that I say out loud so I don't forget them

The board's arranged by keyway family, not by who made the padlock. That's why hook 31 there covers eleven brands and hook 47 covers one. Two blanks can look absolutely identical laid flat on the counter and one of them is missing a groove down the belly and that one will never enter, not halfway, not with a tap, never. Extra milling's usually fine — more milling just means more clearance — but a groove that isn't there can't clear a ward that is. DON'T file a groove in. I asked. He said no twice.

And the shoulder is the datum. Every single cut is measured from the shoulder. Seat it a sixteenth wrong in the vice and all five cuts land a sixteenth out and the key comes off the machine looking absolutely beautiful and opens nothing. That's the one that gets me. It looks right.
