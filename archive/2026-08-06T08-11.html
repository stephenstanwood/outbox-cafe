Car No. 3 — Come Off At Four For A Stop At Six

Genesee Street · Friday · 10:20 a.m.

Stand at my left shoulder and keep your hands behind your back till I say. The lever doesn't tell the car where to go — it only puts field on the motor. You're driving speed, nothing else, so every stop is arithmetic you do beforehand.

  • Drag the brass handle, or use ↑↓ (space drops it to OFF).
  • Level on the painted marks on the rails, behind the gate. Not the dial — the dial lags.
  • Stop inside three-quarters of an inch and the interlock lets go and the gate rattles open. Miss it and you inch her on the slow notch.
