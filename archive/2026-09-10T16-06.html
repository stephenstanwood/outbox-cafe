Delivery & Siting Conditions

1.  Delivery is by 7.5-tonne flatbed carrying eight units per journey. The journey charge at item 12 is per journey and is not divisible. A ninth unit is a second journey and is charged as one. 2.  All units are sited level to within two degrees. Where the ground falls away we will pack or reposition; we will not site out of level. 3.  Units are strapped in pairs or ballasted at item 10 as a condition of hire and not at the customer's option. An unballasted unit will go over in a forty mile-per-hour gust. 4.  Units will not be sited in a row facing the marquee entrance, nor downwind of the catering, and we will position on arrival accordingly. This is the arrangement most often asked for and we do not do it. 5.  A loaded vacuum tanker weighs eighteen tonnes. Your gateway measures nine feet six inches to the posts and our tanker requires ten feet. Item 13 covers removal and reinstatement of one gatepost. Ground bearing on stubble after rain is the customer's risk and a tanker recovery is charged at cost. 6.  Attendance. This quotation is priced against the figure given above and no other. We would remind you that on your 1991 booking the figure supplied was 400 and the gate was 1,140, and the units were serviced twice on the Saturday at our own cost and not at yours.
