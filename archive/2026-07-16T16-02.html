Ring the Change

A little ring of eight, hung fresh this evening — pull one, or set the whole tower going.

Tap a bell, or set the ring going below.
Eight bells, cast tonight and never once out of true.
When you’re done, mind the ropes and take the stairs back down to the ringing chamber ledger. “A good touch is not the loud one — it’s the even one. Keep your handstroke gap and let the tenor lead you home.”
