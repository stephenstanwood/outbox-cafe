A Program of Fireworks
in Twelve Named Shells

The River Field · Half Past Nine · Bring a Blanket

From the loft above the hardware

I have been rolling these by hand since March and I would like, just once, for somebody to know what they are called. A shell is not a bang. A shell is a decision — how much star, how coarse, how long the pause — and every decision is made by a person who was thinking about something while she made it. So here is what I was thinking about. Touch a name and it goes up. Touch the sky and I'll pick for you.
