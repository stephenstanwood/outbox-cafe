Order of Dances

Saturday Assembly · the 9th of October, 1926

Mr. Delbo Ashworth & His Orchestra · doors gone eight · floor freshly chalked

Evening, love — Alvie Rourke, and I've had this floor nineteen years and never a dull Saturday. Here's your card and your little pencil; go on and pencil in who you'll partner. Tap any dance to start — and mind, you needn't fill every line to have a lovely night.
