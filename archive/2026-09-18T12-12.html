FLOOR TIMES — THURSDAY THROUGH SUNDAY

        

          

          

          

        

        

          Couples comes up every forty minutes. Backwards follows it.
          The floor guard will pull a skater who is carrying more speed than the
          crowd around him. That is not about the child. Two hundred beginners
          all doing the same speed is a safe floor; one skater doing double is not,
          and it is the difference that does it, not the speed.
        

      


      

        
HOUSE RULES

        

          
    
            
  1. Skate counterclockwise. Always. The guard will turn you.
    2. 
            
  2. No food, no pop, no gum on the maple. The floor is sealed urethane over hard rock maple and sugar is the worst thing that can land on it.
    3. 
            
  3. Walk the carpet strip from the rental counter to the floor gate. Do not walk on your wheels across the concrete. The strip is there so you don't and it is the single best thing we do.
    4. 
            
  4. NO SPEED SKATING DURING ALL-SKATE. NO EXCEPTIONS AND NO ARGUING WITH THE GUARD AT THE RAIL.
    5. 
            
  5. Children under six stay on the beginner half until a guard says otherwise.
    6. 
            
  6. DO NOT SIT ON THE RAIL. DO NOT HANG ON THE RAIL. THE RAIL IS NOT FOR SITTING ON.

  7. Rental skates stay in the building. Returned at the counter, not left on a bench.
