Scorecard Key — 100 Points

APPEARANCE — jar, pack, headspace, clean rim, clear liquid, no floaters, label placement20 COLOR — natural to the fruit, even top to bottom, no darkening at the shoulder15 TEXTURE & SET — correct set for the class, fruit held in suspension, no weeping, no syneresis at the edge, pickles firm and not translucent25 FLAVOR — characteristic of the fruit, sugar and acid in balance, no scorch, no metallic finish, spice not covering40 TOTAL100
BLUE — 90 and above  ·  RED — 80 to 89  ·  WHITE — 70 to 79  ·  below 70, no placing.
Ties are broken on the FLAVOR column, and if the flavor column ties as well, on TEXTURE & SET. It has never gone past that here.
DISQUALIFICATION, BEFORE THE LID EVER COMES OFF: headspace out of range for the pack; a chipped or sticky sealing rim; a ring left on and rusted to the lid; a jar not sealed; PARAFFIN OF ANY KIND, which has not been accepted in this or any class since the rules changed and will not be accepted next year either. A disqualified jar is set at the end of the table with a yellow slip and its exhibitor is welcome to come ask me why, and several of you have.

Fresh spoon every single taste, rinsed between, and Ruth Ann keeps the water glass at her elbow — that is why judging runs three and a half hours for sixty-one jars and not one hour. If you were watching from the rope and wondering what was taking so long, that was what.

DEMONSTRATION AT TWO O’CLOCK at the demonstration table, back of the pavilion: Ruth Ann on testing a set without a thermometer, and on why your strawberry floats. Bring a chair from the stack if the benches fill, they will.

No. 111DORIS SWEHLA — State CenterRED86

(Doris has taken second in this class four years running and I will not be the one to tell her about her headspace. Her jam is the best jam on that table and the card does not have a column for that. Doris, the fruit floats because it goes in hot and the syrup goes in hotter — stand it five minutes and stir it down once before you fill and you will take this class next August.)
No. 106MARY JO TIEDEMANN — RhodesWHITE78
Judges: “Weeping at the edge, approximately 1/8 in. of free liquid standing when the jar was tipped. Deducted under Texture & Set. Color and flavor otherwise agreeable.”
(A spread that weeps loses points even when it tastes better than the one above it. That is not a judge being fussy, that is twenty-five points sitting right there on the card in ink. Weeping is under-cooked pectin or too little acid, and it is the most fixable thing in this whole building.)
