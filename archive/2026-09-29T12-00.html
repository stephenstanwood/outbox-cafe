18 of the World's Greatest Road Junctions — at Scale! (Roughly!)
The grass verge by Car Park D2, Silbury Boulevard, Central Milton Keynes · Open 10am till the light goes · Adults £1.20 · Under-12s 70p · Putters & pencils from the kiosk

Hello and welcome! Every hole on this course is a real road junction from somewhere in the world, built by me out of paving slabs, marine ply and a very great deal of paint. Your ball is a vehicle, so treat it kindly, give way to the right, and have a smashing afternoon! Six strokes max, then pick up and write 7. Hole-in-one? Come and tell me and I'll ring the bell! — Clive O-B. (ret'd, County Highways, 31 years!)
