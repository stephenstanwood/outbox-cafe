First — the friendly part

Hello and welcome! You have arrived at the fourteenth stop on our little ring, which means somebody clicked a green button somewhere and thought of us. That's lovely. Thank you.

Here is what we do here, said plainly and with a smile: we watch one cloud. Not clouds. A cloud. It has been over the Kettleback Ridge, thirty-one kilometres northeast of town, since the afternoon of June 4, 1997, and it has not gone anywhere, and it does not appear on any weather product we have been able to obtain, and we have obtained a great many.

We are not upset about this. Please don't be upset either! It's a very pretty cloud. But we do keep records, and we do think somebody should be told, and the appropriate somebodies have all been extremely polite and unhelpful, so — the ring. Hi. ☺

Quick FAQ (we get asked a lot, and that's okay!)

Q: Isn't that just a lenticular cloud?
A: A wonderful guess! Lenticulars form and dissolve. Ours has a freckle — a small darker patch, lower right — and the freckle has been in the same place for twenty-nine years. Clouds do not have freckles. We adore it anyway.

Q: Have you asked the airport?
A: Yes! Three times! They were so nice. Their radar shows clear sky over Kettleback. Their pilots, when asked in person and off the record and after a beer, say "oh, the shelf." They call it the shelf. Nobody wrote that down officially. We wrote it down. ☺

Q: Are you okay?
A: We're great! Thanks for checking. That's kind.

A gentle note about the other stops

Some of our neighbours on the ring get a bit heated. We're not like that here. If you've come from #13 (the fellow with the pipeline maps) — he's a dear, he means well, and he is wrong about the reservoir. If you're heading to #15 — bring patience, it loads slowly, and Marguerite hand-codes everything herself at 74, which we think is fantastic.

We don't know what our cloud is. We're not going to pretend we do. We just think somebody ought to have written it down, so we did, every day, for twenty-nine years, with a smile. ☺
