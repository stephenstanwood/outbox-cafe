This Week's Muffin · Plum-Cardamom, and a Word About Patience

This week's muffin is plum-cardamom, and I want to say right away that I approve. It is late September, the light through the bay window has gone that honey color, and a plum muffin is exactly the right thing to be eating while the maple across the street makes up its mind. Somebody back there is paying attention to the calendar. I notice these things.

Now, the particulars. The crumb is tender, with a little give, the way a good cushion ought to be. The plums are cut in generous pieces, not minced into nothing, so you get a proper purple pocket every few bites. The cardamom is where I have notes. It is present, but it is shy. Cardamom should walk in and introduce itself, not hover by the coat rack. One more pinch, I think. I am saying this because I love this place.

My late husband Roy did crossword puzzles every Sunday morning, in pen, which he considered a matter of character. He used to say that the best clues are the ones that make you wait a moment before the answer arrives. That is how I feel about a spiced muffin. The fruit comes first, and then, if the baker has been brave, the warmth follows along a beat behind. Roy would have finished this muffin before he finished seven-across, and he would have told me so.

My cousin Frederick came by on Thursday and declared the muffin "cinematic." Frederick says this about most things, including a folding chair, on account of his time as an extra in something he will not name. I let him have it. Eugene, meanwhile, has relocated from the lobby to the corner above the radio, where he appears to be enjoying the Saturday afternoon program. He is still welcome.

So: four out of five whiskers, with a fifth waiting on the cardamom. Get one while the plums are good. They do not stay good forever, and neither does September.

—Doris
