This Week's Muffin · Peach and Brown Butter

Well. It is July, and the peaches have arrived, and I want you to know I take that seriously.

This week's offering is a peach muffin with brown butter, and I will tell you plainly that it is one of the better things this kitchen has done all summer. The peach is real peach — not a rumor of peach, not a whisper — but actual soft, jammy fruit that gives a little when you press it, the way a good afternoon gives a little when you stop rushing it. The brown butter does that nutty, toasted thing along the edges. My cousin Frederick, who claims to know things about pastry on account of a summer he says he spent near a bakery, would call it "sophisticated." I would just call it good.

The top is where they got it right. A proper muffin needs a domed top with a bit of crackle, and this one has it. Roy used to say the same about his birdhouses — that the roof is the part people notice, so you don't skimp on the roof. He built a dozen of them one spring. Only Eugene the lobby spider ever moved into one, and he prefers the corner by the window, but the sentiment held.

My one quibble, and I offer it gently because I love this place: they are a touch shy on the salt. A pinch more would let the sweetness stand up straight. The weather has been thick and warm all week, the kind of heat that makes the radio sound lazy, and a little salt cuts right through that. Small thing. Fixable thing.

Sit in the window with one of these and a long cool drink and watch the street go by slow. That is the correct way to eat a peach muffin. A kitten named Mortimer wandered through Thursday and did not stay for one, which was his loss entirely.

Get them while the peaches are honest. They will not be for long.

—Doris
