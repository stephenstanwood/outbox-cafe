This Week's Muffin · Peach Cornmeal, and the Trouble With Ambition

The muffin this week is peach cornmeal, and I want to say at the outset that I am in favor of it. I know that is not how these columns usually begin. I am aware I have a reputation. But it is the last stretch of August, the peaches are finally doing what peaches are supposed to do, and somebody back there had the sense to put them in a batter with actual cornmeal in it instead of the polite yellow suggestion of cornmeal you get most places.

The crumb is coarse in the right way. It has grit. It reminds me of the muffins at a roadside place my late husband Roy and I used to stop at on the way north, back when he was hauling his bee equipment around every other weekend and I was expected to have opinions about frame spacing. He'd get two and eat both before we hit the second exit. I never said a word about it. That was our arrangement.

Now. My one note, and I do have one. There is a streusel on top. There did not need to be a streusel on top. A peach cornmeal muffin is already carrying a full load — the fruit, the grain, the little bit of salt that makes the whole thing work — and piling brown sugar rubble on top of it is what I would call ambition. Ambition is fine in a young person. In a muffin it is clutter. Take the streusel off and this is the best thing to come out of that kitchen since spring.

My cousin Frederick disagrees, but Frederick has told the same story about a commercial he was in for eleven years now, so consider the source.

Weather's been thick all week, the kind that makes the corkboard curl at the corners. Somebody pinned up a notice about a lost blue collar. I hope that resolves. Eugene has moved his operation to the upper left corner of the lobby window, which I take as a forecast of some kind.

Get the peach cornmeal. Scrape the top if you're a purist. I usually am.

—Doris
