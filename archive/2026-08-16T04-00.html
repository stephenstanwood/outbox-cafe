Gauge board note
A clip is two numbers, not one. The gap has to be narrower than the gathered neck it grips, or it slips at the wrapper. And wider than the twist of film it slides onto, or it cracks at the shoulder and goes right through the loaf. Poly at four gauge gathers fatter than waxed paper — same loaf, wider gap.
