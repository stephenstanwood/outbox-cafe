THE WHOLE TOBOGGAN

Six matching crew-neck sweaters for one sled team, and a tube for the dog. Same red-and-white yoke on everyone; a different motif on each chest, chosen for the job.

Thank you for buying this leaflet. (Thank you. I mean it.) It is my first. I have checked every row four times. — Mlle. Thérèse Okonkwo-Dufresne

Cover photograph: the toboggan team in No. 412. (The dog is facing the wrong way. I am so sorry. The photographer is sorry too.)

Materials

Needles: No. 8 (and a set of No. 6 for the ribbing). Tension: 18 sts and 22 rows = 4 in. over colourwork. Please knit a square first. (I know nobody wants to. I didn't either.)

Abbreviations. W — frost white. R — cranberry red. K — knit. P — purl. RS — right side. Bsl — work as for Basil.

The Charts

Choose a motif. Click the chart (or press UP, or SPACE) to count off a row; the chart-keeper slides up and the swatch knits itself. DOWN takes a row back. Press F to turn the swatch over and see the floats. Keys 1–6 change motif. (Please do not worry about the floats. They are meant to be there.)
