official & complete rules for the game we play behind the rec center, as decided in the air, one flip at a time!!!

OKAY so here is the thing and I need you to understand this before you scroll one more inch: nobody wrote these rules down first. There was no meeting!! There was no guy with a clipboard!! There was a coin, and there were eight of us, and there was a question, and the coin went UP and it came DOWN and that was that FOREVER!!!

things already settled, do not re-flip, we are begging you

These came down YEARS ago and they came down HARD and we are not litigating them in 1999!!

  • Eight players!! Nine if the ninth one is quiet about it.
  • The grass must have been mowed twice and both times recently. Once-mowed grass is a totally different game and we do not know its rules.
  • There is a heavy ball and a light ball. Only one of them is in play at a time and it is never the one you think.
  • The far pole is the pole. The near pole is a pole. HUGE difference!!
  • After sunset the heavy ball may not touch a hand. Elbows, knees, foreheads, jackets: all fine!! Hands: NO!!
  • The dead patch in the northeast corner is worth double and everybody knows why and nobody says why.
  • We do not have a name for the game. That's rule zero and rule zero has never been flipped.
