A bag of BURSTIES™ Cosmic Ranch weighed 1.1 ounces and contained, without exception, seven puffs of extruded corn, dusted in a buttermilk-onion powder the company called Cosmic and the rest of us call The Wash. Production ended without announcement in March 2001. No successor exists. What follows is kept freely, by many hands, in the plural voice we agreed on.

▸ 11:14 pm — oldmachine_dev — "what the wash is actually for"
A correction, kindly meant, for the newer members who keep writing that The Wash was "just seasoning." Seasoning is applied to be tasted. The Wash was applied to be carried. Consider: a bag opened at 6:50am left a residue that survived a shower, two classes, and a bus ride, and could be found under the nail at supper. Nothing else sold in that corridor did that. The company sold a flavor. The corridor received a marker. Whoever ate one was findable all day by anyone who knew to look at hands.

Three of us met that way in 1998 without exchanging a word about it. That is the whole doctrine, honestly. The rest is scheduling.

the seven, as recited at the glass

  1. THE FLOOR — the coin drops farther than expected. Let it.
  2. THE TURN — the coil is slower than desire and that is a mercy.
  3. THE HEAT — a bag near the compressor comes out warm. Warm is a gift and not a defect.
  4. THE SEAM — opened from the corner, never torn across, or the seven scatter.
  5. THE ASKING — say the name aloud to somebody, even now, especially now.
  6. THE WASH — worn, not licked. Carried until evening.
  7. THE EMPTY BAG — folded in three and kept in the left pocket until laundry decides.
Recitation takes ninety seconds. Nobody has ever gotten through it in a busy corridor without stopping for someone to pass.
