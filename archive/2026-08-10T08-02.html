Nine Skips and the Heron Looks Up

A flat stone, a low sun, and the whole lake holding still for you.

Tap the water once to set your angle, again to set your power. Low and fast is the secret. Everyone at this lake knows it and nobody says it out loud.

Stones are on loan from the bank and must be returned by the water, in the water, at speed.
other evenings, same lake
Skipping season runs from the first warm Thursday until the reeds go brown. The heron abstains from judging beginners, mostly.
