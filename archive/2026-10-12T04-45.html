IN A WORLD...
WHERE THE 4TH IS CHASING THE 5TH...

You are the 5th. Uncle Royce has the flu. You learned the line on a napkin.

SPACE or click: hold, then let go. A short hold is a soft pull and the bell rings late. A long hold sets the bell with a clunk, and then you need a gentle pull-off. Strike in your slot, right after the bell you follow. Keys 1–8 flash a rope. Hold H for the chalk line.

Click or tap the picture to pull. Hold, then release. The number buttons flash a rope so you can learn whose you follow. Sound needs the first click.
